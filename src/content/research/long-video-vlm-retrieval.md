## Problem

Locate relevant moments in a long video using a text query. Coarse retrieval can narrow the search; a stronger vision-language model can inspect candidate frames and surrounding context.

## Questions

How do motion and scene similarity affect false matches? Which temporal window provides enough evidence? How should hard negatives be selected? Add datasets, retrieval metrics and ablations after verification.
